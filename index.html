Why it’s different

  • Your files never leave your device

    Nothing is uploaded to a server. Assistant downloads its model only after you agree.

  • No subscription, no accounts

    Free to use. Open an app and start working, with nothing to sign up for.

  • Works offline

    After an app’s first visit, it keeps working without a connection.

  • One backup file

    Files saves everything your apps store in this browser as a single .backup file.
