FONTLOG for the ITpris font family

Basic Font Information
----------------------

ITpris is the type family of ITpris Verktyg: a slightly narrow sans and a mono made from open-source (SIL OFL) fonts, with full Swedish coverage (å ä ö, §, « », „ ”), round dots, an open K, a straight-tailed y and a G with a spur, tabular figures by default.

Version 1.000. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).

Styles and their sources
------------------------

- ITpris Sans: modified from Instrument Sans (Copyright 2022 The Instrument Sans Project Authors (https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans)).
    Axes: wdth fixed at 92, wght 400-700.
    Upstream alternates made the default: ss01, ss03, ss04, ss07, tnum.
    ss01: Square dots.
    ss02: Classic y.
    ss03: Classic K.
    ss04: Classic G.
    ss05: Proportional figures.
    ss06: Single-storey a.
    Renamed (OFL 1.1); kerning, marks and the other kept features as upstream.
- ITpris Mono: modified from Red Hat Mono (Copyright 2024 The Red Hat Project Authors (https://github.com/RedHatOfficial/RedHatFont)).
    Axes: wght 300-700.
    Upstream alternates made the default: ss01, ss02.
    The dots of i and j redrawn: circle.
    ss01: Double-storey a.
    ss02: Classic g.
    Renamed (OFL 1.1); kerning, marks and the other kept features as upstream.
