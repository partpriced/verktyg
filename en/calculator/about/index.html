About Calculator

Calculator is a calculator that keeps a paper tape of what you worked out. It handles brackets, percentages, powers, roots and units, and currency with exchange rates you enter yourself. It also does date and time arithmetic, such as 2026-01-31 + 1 month. You can save a calculation to Files, or add a date result to Calendar. Nothing is uploaded.
