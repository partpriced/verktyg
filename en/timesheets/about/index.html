About Timesheets

Timesheets records the time you work: start the timer when you begin, or type the time as 1:30, 1.5 or 90m. Each entry has a customer, an optional project and an hourly rate, and time can be billable or internal. The list totals your hours for this week, last week or any month, with the billable value in each currency, and exports CSV. When it is time to bill, choose the customer, tick the hours, and Timesheets makes a draft invoice in Invoices with one line per entry. Billed time is locked so the invoice and the timesheet always agree; void the invoice and the time is free to bill again. Everything stays in this browser, and nothing is uploaded.
