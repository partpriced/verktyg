About World Clock

World Clock shows the time in the cities you choose, works out the time difference between two zones, and plans a meeting across zones by finding the hours when everyone is inside working hours. A planned meeting can be added to Calendar as a meeting. The clock follows your device, with a manual offset you can set. Nothing is uploaded and nothing syncs from the network.
