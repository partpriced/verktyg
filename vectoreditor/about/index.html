Vector Editor

An illustration editor for the browser. Pen, shapes, type, layers and Pathfinder booleans, with SVG, PDF and .ai export.

Open Vector Editor Back to Files

What it does

Draw

Use the pen and pencil, live shapes and type. The direct selection tool edits individual points on a path.

Layers and booleans

Organise objects in layers. Pathfinder operations combine shapes into new paths, with real geometry rather than a stack of masks.

Guides and snapping

Rulers drag out guides. Guide layouts apply in one undo step, and snapping lines objects up to guides when you move them.

Export

Save SVG, PNG, PDF and .ai files. The PDF is written by the app itself, with no library in the page.

Send and save

Save to Files on this device and send an SVG or PNG to another ITpris Verktyg app, such as Image Editor.

Privacy

Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts. Your drawings stay in this browser, and Files keeps them on this device. No tracking runs unless the owner has turned it on in Settings.

What it cannot do yet

  • The single-file offline download is currently broken by a module script tag that the offline builder cannot inline. The hosted app works. A fix is in progress.
  • Changing the fill colour, font, size or alignment of a text object may not update the canvas. This is a known bug being checked.
  • Double-clicking a layer does not open an options dialog yet, so per-layer fills, strokes, drop shadows and blur effects are not available.
  • Double-clicking text on the canvas does not edit it yet, and double-clicking a group does not enter isolation mode.
